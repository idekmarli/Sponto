## Recommended AI Tools

1. **Midjourney** - Best for stylized, artistic icons
2. **DALL-E 3** - Good for specific, literal interpretations  
3. **Ideogram** - Excellent for text integration
4. **Adobe Firefly** - Good for commercial-safe assets
5. **Figma AI** - For quick icon variations

## Post-Processing

After generation, use:
- **Vectorizer.ai** - Convert to SVG for scalability
- **Remove.bg** - Clean up backgrounds
- **Figma/Sketch** - Final adjustments and export at all sizes
